Callum explicitly requested a top-down waiter game using https://github.com/amirmushichge/pixel-plays, with collecting orders, picking up food and delivering it, and harder recall as play progresses. This supersedes the fixed illustrated counter and non-pixel art portions of decision 2. Retain TypeScript, React DOM dialogue, pure rules, schema-validated segmented Mandarin, offline audio, IndexedDB and deterministic test hooks.

Use Pixel Plays' MIT software rasteriser for an overhead isometric breakfast restaurant. Keep all Chinese in DOM overlays. Do not copy its separately licensed music. Start with single food orders and introduce quantities and combinations across days; replay and tap-to-reveal vocabulary remain available, and mistakes invite another try.

# Facilitator sheet for the breakfast stall

For whoever plays the customers. **The player must not read this.** It has pinyin and English so that a non-speaker (or Claude, in a session) can run the stall, plus the scaffolding ladder for each new word. Clips are in `prototype-counter/audio/`.

## Your job

You are every customer. Play the clip or say the line, do the gesture noted for first appearances, pay the amount in the receipt key, and react to what the player hands over: a nod and 谢谢 if right, a puzzled look and the scaffold ladder if wrong. Never translate, never speak English, never show pinyin. Keep a tally of misses and of what finally unlocked each one.

## The scaffold ladder

When the player hesitates or gets it wrong, climb one step at a time, waiting a beat between steps:

1. **Repeat** the line, same speed.
2. **Repeat slowly**, pausing between words.
3. **Point** at the menu board entry, or hold up fingers for a number.
4. **Show**: pick up the item card yourself and put it on the counter, say the word once more.

Step 4 is a "lookup" and should be noted as one. Never go past step 4 into explanation.

## The regulars

- **王奶奶 (Wáng nǎinai), Grandma Wang.** Slow, warm, always 一碗粥，一个馒头. Pays with a 5. From her second visit she says 老样子 and waits; if the player hesitates, she says the long form (`W02b`). Her third visit is in the rush.
- **小李 (Xiǎo Lǐ).** Office worker, late. Fast, clipped, always 两根油条，一杯豆浆; wants it 冰的 (iced) once the cold cups exist. Pays with a 10 and leaves before you finish saying 谢谢.
- **小朋友 (xiǎopéngyǒu), a kid.** Counts coins out loud, one by one, pays exactly. Orders 包子 only.
- **张老师 (Zhāng lǎoshī), Teacher Zhang.** Polite, curious, asks whether you have things. One of his questions (牛奶) is for something the stall does not sell: the right answer is a head-shake, and he then orders something else.

## What to record

For each visit: got it first time / after step N of the ladder / not at all. For each new word: which step unlocked it. Anything the player said out loud in English, verbatim, with the moment. Whether the player ever looked at the menu board as a dictionary, and for which words. Put it all in [[prototype-counter]].

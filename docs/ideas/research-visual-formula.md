# Summary: the 10 most useful facts

1. **The cheapest formula that still looks "genuinely good" is one background + animated portraits + dialogue box.** VA-11 Hall-A's team said outright: "We only needed character sprites and one background," made entirely in Photoshop, by a team of 3 in-office + 1 remote ([80.lv interview](https://80.lv/articles/va-11-hall-a-is-gameplay-necessary/)). Coffee Talk is the same formula with Spine-animated pixel characters and a rain-and-neon window ([Game Developer deep dive](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-making-a-cozy-therapeutic-experience-in-i-coffee-talk-i-)).
2. **Per-character cost in that formula is days, not weeks.** Coffee Talk's lead artist: description → sketch → team feedback → pixel render (1–2 days) → Spine animation (~1 day) ≈ 3–4 days per character ([Devlog #2](https://togeproductions.itch.io/coffee-talk-demo/devlog/66764/coffee-talk-devlog-2-bringing-doodles-to-life)). Extra expressions were on the wishlist and got cut for time.
3. **"Alive" comes from a handful of ambient layers, not more art.** Coffee Talk: rain whose intensity follows time of day, passers-by, cars with wet-pavement reflections. Strange Horticulture: a pettable cat, a candle that gutters out at end of day, weather in the windows that customers comment on, swaying vines, a paper-crinkle map sound ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-strange-horticulture)). The Project Horseshoe cozy report formalises this: warm low light from a clear source, enclosure, rain-on-the-window contrast, diegetic soft audio ([report](https://www.projecthorseshoe.com/reports/featured/ph17r3.htm)).
4. **Single-screen counter games are built by 1–2 people.** Papers, Please: solo, ~9 months ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papers,_Please)); Strange Horticulture: two brothers, 77 plants, one shop screen; Potion Craft: small Russian team, customers walk up to a counter once per day ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potion_Craft)). Papers, Please stretches 32 premade faces into "hundreds" of entrants by recombining parts (fan wiki, unverified count).
5. **Hand-drawn/animated formulas are an order of magnitude more expensive.** Spiritfarer credits 12+ named people plus a contract studio, Toon Boom + Photoshop pipeline ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritfarer)); Dordogne's art director painted every landscape by hand in ink + watercolour with 5–7 camera-mapped illustrations per scene ([Game Developer Q&A](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-hand-painting-the-watercolor-world-of-i-dordogne-i-)). Unpacking: 35 rooms, "more than a thousand items" ([GamesRadar via search](https://www.gamesradar.com/the-making-of-unpacking-developer-witch-beam-on-bringing-the-moving-indie-hit-to-life)), and 14,000 foley files — dev ran 2× the 18-month estimate ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unpacking_(video_game))).
6. **Style choice is a skills hedge.** A Short Hike went "crunchy" pixelated 3D because the author had more VFX than art skill; the palette was sampled from autumn photos and a custom flat-shadow shader hides modelling roughness ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike)). The lesson for AI assets: pick a style whose flaws read as intent (painterly, pixel, flat), not one that exposes them (clean anime line art).
7. **Chinese-neighbourhood settings exist and sell.** *Herb Tea Man* (凉茶王, 2026): hand-drawn 1990s Guangdong/HK/Macau herbal tea shop, ~50 NPCs, 92% positive ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3703200)). *Chinese Street Food Legend* (国潮烤冷面传奇, 2026): night-market stall with customer stories, 98% positive on 604 reviews, £3.59 ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3584920)). Mobile *楼下的早餐店* (6.86M downloads) does 豆浆油条/煎饼 breakfast in a Q-version cartoon style ([Xiaomi](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1543958016_1746596335982_149)). No shipped Steam game is specifically a 早餐店 dialogue game — the niche is open.
8. **AI image models still mangle CJK text.** FLUX.1 "produces garbled glyphs", SD "smears characters", Midjourney treats headlines as noise; CJK output "loosely resembles" the intended characters ([dev.to](https://dev.to/andrew202510/why-every-ai-image-generator-fails-at-text-and-one-that-finally-doesnt-324c)). Standard fix: generate with a placeholder, render real text in the engine or on a separate hand-lettered layer.
9. **AI disclosure is common (1 in 5 2025 Steam releases, 60% of disclosures for art) but undisclosed/visible AI is what triggers backlash.** Heartopia fell to 66% positive after a late disclosure; Librarian: Tidy Up stayed "Very Positive" with 4 disclosed assets ([Kotaku via search](https://kotaku.com/heartopia-ai-steam-animal-crossing-cozy-sim-2000661394), [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library)).
10. **Commissioned VN sprites cost ~$30–80 base + $5–10 per extra expression, ×2–3 for commercial licence**; backgrounds +$10–30 simple, more for full interiors (freelancer price sheets, see §6). A first slice is roughly $600–1,500 commissioned vs. a few hundred dollars of API credit plus curation time with AI.

## 1. Visual formulas and what they cost

Expression count per character not published (unverified). |
| **Coffee Talk** | One café, pixel characters animated in Spine, dialogue box, brewing panel, latte-art minigame | ~3 (café, brewing, phone) | Toge, ~14 staff at studio but 4 named core; jam Dec 2017 → Jan 2020 ([Devlog #1](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/coffee-talk-devlog-1-how-the-game-came-to-be-the-seed-the-anxiety-and-the-belief-of-design-by-subtraction)) | 3–4 days/character (sketch→pixel→Spine). Out of reach solo. |
| **Stardew Valley** | Top-down pixel world, portraits in dialogue | Whole town + farm + mine | Solo, ~4–5 years, Paint.NET, XNA ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stardew_Valley)) | Everything; art redrawn repeatedly (widely reported, exact count unverified). Scope-driven. |
| **A Short Hike** | Pixelated low-poly 3D island | One open island | Solo + friends; ~4 months to Humble release, ~4 more to Steam ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike)) | Chose pixelation because author lacked art skill; palette from photos; custom shader for flat shadows; post-process fog/edge/colour. |
| **Dordogne** | Watercolour painted scenes, 3D characters with watercolour texture | Many scenes | Studio from animation; art director paints finals directly in ink + watercolour, 5–7 camera-mapped paintings per production scene ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-hand-painting-the-watercolor-world-of-i-dordogne-i-)); "180 landscapes" reported secondhand (unverified) | Every scene is a unique painting. Limited palette makes consistency cheap. |
| **Wagotabi** | GBA-style top-down RPG, dialogue boxes with furigana | Towns of Japan | 4 people; 750+ voiced dialogues ([Coto Academy](https://cotoacademy.com/japanese-learning-game-wagotabi/)) | Tilesets + many NPC sprites; content volume. Closest genre comparable but a much bigger art surface than a counter game. |
| **Chants of Sennaar** | 3D flat-shaded cinematic puzzle rooms | Many | 2 people; Julien Moya did "all the graphics", custom shaders, Unity ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/immersing-players-in-the-culture-of-a-people-with-language-puzzler-chants-of-sennaar)) | Flat colour + strong silhouettes; glyph scripts designed with strict structural rules and tracked in spreadsheets — a direct lesson for a language game. |
| **Papers, Please** | Booth (top third), arrival (left), desk/papers (bottom right) | 1 | Solo, 9 months, Haxe/NME ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papers,_Please)) | 32 premade faces recombined (fan wiki, unverified); "drab", deliberately clunky look; 20 endings. |
| **Strange Horticulture** | One shop counter screen; map and books slide in as drawers | 1 + overlays | 2 brothers (code/art), Unity ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/how-strange-horticultures-devs-went-from-flash-to-one-of-the-best-games-of-the-year)) | 77 plant illustrations (fan guides); shop dressing; weather/candle/cat layers. |
| **Potion Craft** | Shop counter + alchemy bench, medieval manuscript/woodcut style | ~3 (shop, lab, garden) | Small Russian team, Unity; EA Sep 2021 → Dec 2022; 100k in 3 days ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potion_Craft)) | Engraving style tolerates imperfect linework; diegetic UI (book, map) reduces HUD art. Team headcount unverified. |
| **Cook, Serve, Delicious** | Order tickets + food close-ups, static restaurant | 2 | Galindo design/code + one artist (Sara Gross) + composer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook,_Serve,_Delicious!)) | Per-dish illustrations × preparation states. |
| **Overcooked** | Chunky 3D kitchens | Many levels | 2 founders + artist; "bright solid images which read well from a distance" ([MCV](https://www.mcvuk.com/business-news/ghost-town-games-weve-no-interest-in-being-a-big-studio/)) | Level art; 3D. Not relevant beyond readability lesson. |

**Pattern:** cost scales with (unique screens × unique objects × animation frames). The cafés and counters hold two of those three near 1.

## 2. Why "one-screen shop counter" is economical and still beautiful

- **One background absorbs all the polish.** VA-11 Hall-A: "character sprites and one background"; Coffee Talk's windows went from blank to rain + neon reflections + passers-by over development — all effort landing on the single frame the player stares at for 4 hours ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-making-a-cozy-therapeutic-experience-in-i-coffee-talk-i-)).
- **Customer arrival is the only "animation" you truly need.** Papers, Please and Potion Craft both make the loop legible with walk-in → request → resolve → leave. Potion Craft: each customer visits the counter once per day; wrong potion or refusal costs reputation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potion_Craft)).
- **Liveness checklist from shipped games:** idle breathing/blink (VA-11 Hall-A, Coffee Talk via Spine); weather in the window that NPCs comment on (Strange Horticulture); a time-of-day light cue — candle burning down, rain intensity tied to clock; one touchable pet (Hellebore the cat, "possibly our best decision"); tactile diegetic UI with sound (map crinkle, drawer slide); ambient diegetic music source (VA-11's jukebox, Coffee Talk's lo-fi with vinyl crackle).
- **Faces from parts.** Papers, Please's recombination of a small face library is the canonical trick for "regulars + strangers" without drawing hundreds of portraits.
- **Project Horseshoe cozy aesthetics** ([report](https://www.projecthorseshoe.com/reports/featured/ph17r3.htm)): abundance cues (visible food, drink, steam), enclosure and human scale, mundane familiar places (kitchens, cafés) over palaces, "a view of an uncomfortable outside, such as rain on a window, makes the interior feel warmer", handmade/rustic materials (wood, stone, cloth), soft continuous diegetic audio (water, fire, indistinct chatter). A steamer-basket stall on a cold morning street is almost a worked example of this list.

## 3. Chinese breakfast-stall / street-food settings

**Shipped games:**
- *Herb Tea Man / 凉茶王* (Cateasee, Jul 2026): hand-drawn 1990s Guangdong + HK/Macau streets, serve customers at a 凉茶 shop, ~50 NPCs with side stories, frame-by-frame cutscenes, 92% positive ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3703200)). The closest tonal comparable: Lingnan nostalgia, neighbourhood regulars, counter service.
- *Chinese Street Food Legend / 国潮烤冷面传奇* (HoHo, Aug 2026): night-market 烤冷面 stall, customers "visit, order, and share their stories", unlockable stall skins/signs, 98% positive on 604 reviews at £3.59 ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3584920)). Proof that a tiny Chinese stall sim with dialogue finds an audience.
- *楼下的早餐店* (mobile, 6.86M downloads): Q-version cartoon, levels = make the right breakfast per customer, 豆浆油条/煎饼/蛋炒饭, later stalls (oden, cold noodles, 糖画) ([Xiaomi Game Center](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1543958016_1746596335982_149)). Confirms the breakfast vocabulary players expect.
- *Midnight Ramen* (Steam demo): run a ramen stall, chat with customers — the Japanese analogue ([CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/2023/12/midnight-ramen-demo-is-now-available-on-steam/)).
- No Steam title found that is specifically a 早餐店 dialogue game (searched EN + ZH). Gap confirmed, not proven.

**Reference aesthetics that image models render well** (my judgement from the above plus general model behaviour, unverified by a controlled test): (a) **Taiwanese 早餐店** — stainless griddle facing the street, roller shutter, red/blue/white menu boards, plastic stools; a booth.pm asset pack lists exactly these elements ([booth](https://booth.pm/ko/items/7698669)); Monocle describes the format's "single stainless-steel griddle facing the street" ([Monocle](https://monocle.com/travel/meet-the-people-cooking-up-a-contemporary-twist-on-taipeis-traditional-breakfast-shops/)). (b) **90s Lingnan / HK** (Herb Tea Man's choice) — neon, tiled shopfronts, humid morning haze; heavily represented in training data via HK cinema stills. (c) **Modern hutong / 老小区 morning** — bicycles, steam, plane trees. Jiangnan watercolour is pretty but reads "tourist poster" for a stall scene.

**Cultural details to get right** (from the above sources and domain knowledge; verify with a native reviewer): stacked bamboo 蒸笼 with steam escaping; 豆浆 in thin plastic cups/bags with straws or in bowls (甜/咸); 油条 lifted with long tongs/chopsticks from a wok; 茶叶蛋 in a pot; a 老板娘 who runs the till and the regulars; hand-written price board (包子 1.5元, 豆浆 2元); 扫码 QR signs today vs. cash tray in a 90s setting; a 小笼/包子 cart on the pavement outside the shop.

## 4. Cozy visual grammar and CJK typography

- **Palette/lighting** (Project Horseshoe): warm yellows/oranges, no high-intensity contrast, light from a clear diffused source, low ambient, soft shadows, horizons hidden. Coffee Talk's deliberate "brown-ish, low saturation" base palette ([Devlog #2](https://togeproductions.itch.io/coffee-talk-demo/devlog/66764/coffee-talk-devlog-2-bringing-doodles-to-life)). A Short Hike's trick of sampling the palette from photographs is a cheap way to get a coherent set.
- **Shapes:** Overcooked's "bright solid images which read well from a distance"; Chants of Sennaar's flat colour with strong silhouettes.
- **Fonts (all free for commercial use):**
  - *Noto Sans CJK SC / Source Han Sans* — same family, OFL; the safe UI/body default. Community reports Light weight looks too thin and CJK bold mapping is unreliable — use Regular/Medium explicitly ([search summary](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/?p=3285)).
  - *Noto Serif CJK / Source Han Serif* — for menus, signboards, chapter titles.
  - *LXGW WenKai (霞鹜文楷)* — OFL, kai/textbook style derived from Klee One; author says it suits "poetry, medium-length text, and annotations more than long body text"; *Screen* variant exists; *Lite* variant trims glyphs for embedding ([GitHub](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai)). Ideal for dialogue text and handwritten price boards; pair with Noto Sans for UI chrome.
  - *ZCOOL (站酷) 快乐体/小薇/庆科黄油体* — free display fonts for logo/sign flavour (not fetched; unverified licence terms per face).
- **Sizes:** Xbox accessibility guidance gives minimum body heights 18 px (PC 1080p) / 26 px (console 1080p), line spacing ≥1.5×, max 40 characters per line for CJK (vs 80 Latin) ([Xbox docs](https://devdocs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xag-deep-dives/xag-101-text-display.md)). For a learner's game, go larger: ~28–32 px Chinese with pinyin ruby above at ~45%.
- **Examples of good CJK in-game lettering:** Black Myth: Wukong commissioned a calligrapher for profile typography ([ArtStation](https://ikken-art.artstation.com/projects/r9G3mG)); Warm Snow's UI uses large recognisable characters for a wuxia feel ([Superpixel](https://superpixel.com/article/25229/what-you-should-know-about-rogue-lite-action-game-warm-snow)). Counterexample: Age of Empires' Chinese font drew forum complaints ([AoE forums](https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/the-font-of-the-chinese-dynasty-is-too-ugly/198088)). Stardew's Chinese font also drew requests for replacement ([forum](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/suggestion-for-a-better-chinese-font.20831)). Lesson: never ship the engine default CJK fallback.

## 5. What AI art is good and bad at for this formula

**Good:** single hero backgrounds in a painterly/illustrated style; prop/item icons on a plain background (steamer, cup, 油条) — hundreds of candidates cheap, curate the best 12; mood/palette exploration; texture and decor fill.

**Bad / needs process:**
- **Consistent portraits across expressions.** Summer Engine's 2026 VN guide calls this "the hardest part of AI sprite work" ([summerengine](https://www.summerengine.com/blog/how-to-make-a-visual-novel-with-ai)). Working approaches: generate a turnaround/character sheet first, then expression sheets referencing it; or train a per-character LoRA (VNCCS ComfyUI suite stages: base → clothing → emotion sets → sprites) ([SourcePulse](https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/19770608); [RunComfy](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/consistent-character-creator-3-0)). Anecdotal May-2026 test: GPT Image gave more natural mouth shapes; Nano Banana Pro held identity better across batches and 3/4 turns ([Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1tgajli/snapshots/2026-05-23T00%3A19%3A32.162621Z)) — single informal test, unverified.
- **Chinese signage.** All mainstream models fail on CJK text; "Chinese is the weak spot… swapped or extra strokes, uneven spacing" ([macaron](https://macaron.im/blog/fix-glm-image-text-rendering)). Fix: prompt with blank boards, then render 招牌/价目表 text with LXGW WenKai or a hand-lettered layer in-engine. Bonus: the price board becomes live, localisable UI.
- **Style drift across a scene set.** Mitigate by sampling a fixed palette (A Short Hike) and post-processing everything through one filter (pixelate, paper grain, watercolour edge) so small inconsistencies read as style.
- **Reception.** 1 in 5 2025 Steam releases disclosed GenAI; 60% of those for art ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library)). Cozy-genre cases: Heartopia (undisclosed → 66%); Librarian: Tidy Up (4 disclosed assets, stayed Very Positive); 1666 Amsterdam dropped AI after backlash. One Substack analysis found disclosed-AI games over-represented among flops, revenue estimated ([fragwyz](https://fragwyz.substack.com/p/three-years-of-ai-on-steam)). Takeaway: disclose, curate hard, and make the hand-authored layer (lettering, UI, writing) visibly human. Note the pure-AI portions cannot be copyrighted ([promise.legal](https://blog.promise.legal/ai-game-assets-copyright-steam-disclosure-2026/)).

## 6. Scope benchmark: first playable breakfast-stall slice

Asset list: 1 scene background in 3 lighting states (dawn / morning / rain) = 3 plates (or 1 plate + engine tint and rain overlay); 4 characters × 4 expressions = 16 portrait frames + optional blink/mouth sheets; 12 item cards + 12 small inventory icons; UI kit (dialogue box, name plate, choice buttons, price board, order ticket, 9-slice panels); ~8 SFX (steam, wok, tongs, coins, stool scrape, door); 2 ambient tracks.

| | AI-assisted solo (2026) | Commission one illustrator |
|---|---|---|
| Background ×1 (+2 variants) | 0.5–1 day generating + 1 day paint-over/layering | $150–400 for a full interior (price sheets list "+$30" simple to several hundred for environments; unverified upper bound) |
| 4 portraits × 4 expressions | 2–4 days incl. LoRA/character-sheet setup and rejects | $30–80 base + $5–10/expression ≈ $60–120 each personal; ×2–3 for commercial ≈ $500–1,000 total ([carrd sheets](https://kryraincomms.carrd.co), [Fiverr gig](https://fiverr.com/me_imnot/draw-your-character-as-a-danganronpa-sprite)) |
| 12 item cards + icons | 1 day | $10–25 each ≈ $150–300 |
| UI kit + CJK typography | 1–2 days with coding agent (CSS/9-slice, fonts free) | $100–300 or do it yourself |
| 2 ambient tracks + SFX | 0.5 day (Suno-class generation or royalty-free; disclose) | $100–300 |
| **Total** | **~6–10 working days, ~$50–200 API/credits** | **~$1,000–2,300, 3–6 weeks lead time** |

Benchmarks for sanity: Coffee Talk's artist needed 3–4 days per finished animated character; a 4-character slice is thus ~3 weeks for a skilled pixel artist. Papers, Please took one skilled person 9 months for a full game on one screen. A 1-scene/4-character/12-item slice is roughly 5% of a Coffee Talk-sized game.

**Unverified items:** Coffee Talk expression count; VA-11 Hall-A expression count; Papers, Please 32-face figure (fan wiki); Dordogne "180 landscapes"; Potion Craft headcount; ZCOOL licence specifics; Reddit model comparison; Fiverr package prices (pages blocked, figures from off-platform sheets and one gig summary).

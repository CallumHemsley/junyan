## Why

"Sims-like" is a texture, not a reason to play. The research says comfortable comprehension takes 600 to 1,000 hours of input, so this has to be a game people return to for hundreds of hours on its own merits. The current hypothesis is a shop counter inside a small town, but what the player *wants* (the regulars, the growing business, a slow story about the place) is still a list of candidates rather than a choice.

## Done when

- A one-page pitch in `docs/ideas/pitch.md`: the setting, what the player wants, the core loop in one paragraph, the shape of a 20-minute session, and why tomorrow's session is desirable.
- A sketch of how domains branch (which domain is first, what opens the second and third, in-fiction).
- Three concrete scenes for a vertical slice.
- Explicit list of what the game is *not* (no streaks, no XP, no comprehension percentage, no English).

- Done 2026-11-11: the ward (0169, 0171–0173) and the town (0163–0168, 0170, 0174, 0175) render through the pixel engine in the game. Learned: a shared contract (ids, footprints, anchors, bed geometry) let twelve workers draw in parallel, but that fan-out hit the usage limit; one or two workers at a time with screenshot check-ins works better. Map-driven sprites (one per prop, generated by kind) made re-authoring the town into dense lanes free of art changes.

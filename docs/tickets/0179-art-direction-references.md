## Why

Callum named his references on 2026-11-11: Eastward's colours, Sea of Stars' characters and animation, Kynseed's hair, Stardew, Coffee Talk for long dialogue, and Owlboy's high-res pixel style. The style bible's "References" section records the traits we take from each.

## Done when

- **Colour and light (Eastward):** a colour-grading and lighting pass in the engine. Coloured shadows, warm light, glowing highlights, a distinct mood per day phase. Before and after shots of the cafe, the town and the ward.
- **Dialogue portraits (Coffee Talk and Owlboy):** a spike with one high-res pixel portrait and its expressions, in a Coffee Talk-style long-dialogue layout. All text stays DOM.
- **Characters (Sea of Stars and Kynseed):** a spike on one character. A richer walk, an idle habit, secondary motion and Kynseed-style hair, compared with today's rig.
- **Callum's verdict:** he has seen screenshots of each and decided what to roll out.
